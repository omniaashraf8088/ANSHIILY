## 3. Full Description

Welcome to **ANSHIILY (أنشئلي)** — the intelligent graphic design and visual content creation platform engineered for creators, entrepreneurs, and digital marketers worldwide.

Whether you need a high-converting Instagram post, an advertising banner, an elegant executive resume, a company brochure, or a print-ready event flyer; ANSHIILY gives you the power to transform a simple text idea into a fully layered, customizable graphic design in seconds!

---

### ✨ Key Features of ANSHIILY:

#### 1. 🤖 AI-Powered Design Generation Engine
* 5-Step Guided Wizard: Select your category, target platform, visual tone, and creative brief.
* Our AI engine generates structured, multi-layer design documents featuring coordinated typography, shapes, and imagery.

#### 2. 🎨 Multi-Layer Interactive Canvas Editor
* Freeform interactive workspace with intuitive drag, scale, pinch, and rotate gestures.
* Curated Arabic and Latin typography library (Tajawal, Cairo, Poppins) with font size, color, alignment, and shadow controls.
* Complete layer management: bring forward, send backward, duplicate, and delete elements with a tap.
* Built-in Undo and Redo for risk-free creative experimentation.

#### 3. 📂 10 Verified Design Categories & Templates
* **Social Media:** Instagram squares, stories, TikTok thumbnails, Facebook banners.
* **Marketing & Ads:** Promotional banners, sales posters, digital campaigns.
* **Brochures:** Elegant bi-fold and tri-fold corporate brochures.
* **Flyers:** Event flyers, restaurant menus, promotional announcements.
* **Business Documents:** Invoices, executive resumes, official letterheads.
* **Planners & Organizers:** Daily agendas, habit trackers, goal planners.
* **Presentations:** Modern pitch decks and business slide templates.
* **Invitations & Greeting Cards:** Wedding invitations, holiday cards, celebrations.
* **Educational:** Infographics, classroom aids, scientific summaries.
* **Personal:** Custom wallpapers, diary covers, photo keepsakes.

#### 4. 🖨️ Professional 300 DPI Export Engine
* Export in crystal-clear formats: high-res **PNG** with transparency and crisp **JPG**.
* Export commercial print-quality **300 DPI multi-page PDF** files ready for professional offset printing.
* Instant one-tap sharing across messaging apps, email, and social networks.

#### 5. ☁️ Secure Cloud Sync & Bilingual RTL/LTR
* Automatic cloud project saving so you can resume editing anytime, anywhere.
* Native Arabic (Right-to-Left) and English (Left-to-Right) language support with seamless switching.
