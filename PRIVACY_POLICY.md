## 1. Introduction
Welcome to **ANSHIILY ("أنشئلي", "we", "our", or "us")**. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, store, and protect information when you use our mobile application and online services.

---

## 2. Information We Collect

### A. Account Information
- **Registration Data:** When you register, we collect your email address, full name, and display username via Supabase Authentication.
- **Profile Image:** If you choose to upload an avatar, the image is stored in your personal profile directory.

### B. User Content & Design Documents
- **Design Projects & Canvas Elements:** When you create or edit graphic designs, we store your canvas layers, text objects, styling parameters, and document geometry to enable cloud project autosaving and synchronization across your devices.
- **User Uploads & Brand Assets:** Images, logos, and photos that you import into your designs are stored in an isolated, private cloud storage bucket accessible only by your account.
- **Exported Output:** Rendered high-resolution images (PNG, JPG) and PDF files that you generate for download or sharing.

### C. AI Generation Data
- **Design Prompts:** Text descriptions, category selections, and styling preferences you submit to generate AI designs are processed by our backend AI pipeline. Prompts are never sold or used for public advertising.

### D. Subscription & Payment Information
- **Purchase History:** When you subscribe to ANSHIILY Pro, purchases are processed securely through **Google Play In-App Billing** (on Android) or **Apple In-App Purchases** (on iOS).
- **RevenueCat Integration:** We use RevenueCat to verify subscription entitlements (Free vs. Pro). We **never** store or have access to your credit card numbers or banking details.

### E. Diagnostic & Performance Information
- Anonymous crash logs, application version, device model, and OS version to diagnose technical issues and optimize performance.

---

## 3. How We Use Your Information
We use your information exclusively to:
1. Provide, maintain, and personalize the ANSHIILY design editor.
2. Synchronize your projects across mobile and web platforms.
3. Generate customized design documents through our AI engine.
4. Manage account authentication and subscription entitlements.
5. Provide customer support and resolve technical inquiries.
6. Enforce security, prevent fraud, and comply with legal obligations.

---

## 4. Data Sharing & Third-Party Services
We do not sell, rent, or monetize your personal data. We share data only with trusted infrastructure providers required to deliver the service:
- **Supabase (PostgreSQL Cloud):** Cloud database, secure user authentication, and encrypted file storage.
- **RevenueCat:** In-app purchase verification and subscription entitlement management.
- **OpenAI:** Server-side design generation via secure backend Edge Functions.
- **Google Play & Apple:** Payment processing and app distribution.

---

## 5. Data Security & Storage Privacy
- **Encryption:** All communication between your device and our servers uses industry-standard HTTPS / TLS 1.3 encryption.
- **Storage Isolation:** User design files and uploaded brand assets are stored in private storage buckets protected by PostgreSQL Row Level Security (RLS). Other users cannot view or download your private designs.
- **Access Restrictions:** Access to production infrastructure is governed by multi-factor authentication and strict role-based access control (RBAC).

---

## 6. Data Retention & Account Deletion
- **Retention:** We retain your projects and profile data as long as your account remains active.
- **Account Deletion:** You have the right to delete your account and all associated designs, uploads, and profile information at any time.
  - To request account deletion within the app: Navigate to **Profile > Settings > Delete Account**.
  - To request deletion via email: Contact us at `privacy@anshiily.com` or `support@anshiily.com` with the subject "Delete My Account". Upon verification, your account and all associated cloud data will be permanently purged within 30 days.

---

## 7. Children's Privacy
ANSHIILY does not knowingly collect personal data from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the European Economic Area). If we become aware that personal data of a child has been collected without parental consent, we will promptly delete it.

We may update this policy periodically to reflect operational, legal, or regulatory changes. We will notify you of material changes through the application or via email.
