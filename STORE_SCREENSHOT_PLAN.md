## 1. Technical Store Specifications

### A. Google Play Store (Phone & Tablet)
- **Minimum Phone Resolution:** 1080 x 1920 px (Aspect ratio 16:9 or 19.5:9).
- **Tablet Resolution:** 7-inch & 10-inch screenshots (Minimum 1200 x 1920 px).
- **Format:** PNG (24-bit, no alpha) or High-Quality JPEG.
- **Recommended Count:** 6 to 8 localized screenshots per language.

### B. Apple App Store Connect
- **6.9" / 6.7" iPhone Display (Required):** 1290 x 2796 px or 1320 x 2868 px.
- **6.5" iPhone Display:** 1284 x 2778 px or 1242 x 2688 px.
- **13" / 12.9" iPad Pro (Required if Universal):** 2048 x 2732 px.
- **Format:** RGB PNG or JPEG, flattening all alpha transparency.

## 3. Production Design Guidelines for Screenshots
1. **Device Mockup Bezels:** Use minimalist gold/titanium phone frames matching the ANSHIILY dark luxury theme (`#0B132B`).
2. **Typography in Banners:** Tajawal Bold for Arabic headlines; Poppins SemiBold for English subtitles.
3. **High Contrast:** Ensure text has at least a 7:1 contrast ratio against the background gradient.
